<div class="chapter">
<h2>The idea</h2>
<div class="body" markdown="1">
An everyday bag that reads as a small sculpture without giving up comfort. One silhouette mixes a geometric volume with convex and concave curves: a crescent cut at the top, and a body that swells and tucks in along a single line.
</div>

<div class="body" markdown="1">
