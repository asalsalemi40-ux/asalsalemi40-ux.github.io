<div class="chapter">
<h2>The problem</h2>
<div class="body" markdown="1">
Studio chairs are bulky, there is nowhere to keep a tablet, headphones or the slate, and so the set gets cluttered. Nothing about the furniture speaks the visual language of film.
</div>
</div>

<div class="chapter">
<h2>The idea</h2>
<div class="body" markdown="1">
Use the clapperboard itself as the form, turn the chair into a small workstation with built-in storage, and keep it foldable and made from recyclable materials to save space in the studio.
</div>
</div>

<div class="chapter">
<h2>Final design</h2>
<div class="body" markdown="1">
Model two keeps the striped panel as an adjustable backrest, adds an integrated pocket for a tablet and headphones, and sizes the seat for sitting in tight spaces on set.
</div>
