<div class="chapter">
<h2>The question</h2>
<div class="body" markdown="1">
A skateboard is sports equipment whose whole purpose depends on movement. Can it leave motion behind and still keep its identity? And can changing what its main parts do turn it into an object that is both decorative and useful?
</div>
</div>

<div class="chapter">
<h2>The idea</h2>
<div class="body" markdown="1">
Motion becomes light. The wheels are the parts that move a skateboard, so they are the parts I replaced: bulbs sit on the trucks where the wheels were. Physical movement is gone, and light takes over as the movement you see. Because the lamps are where the wheels should be, the deck is recognisable at a glance, but it does something new.

My goals were to turn sports equipment into a decorative object, keep the skateboard's form, use light as both the idea and the function, make a piece that hangs indoors, and build it from simple materials that are easy to find.
</div>
</div>

<div class="chapter">
<h2>Building it</h2>
<div class="body" markdown="1">
The deck is drilled for the bulb sockets, the sockets are mounted on the trucks, and the bulbs are wired to a power cord with an inline switch. The light hangs about 5 cm off the wall, so it throws light onto the wall around it.
</div>
</div>
