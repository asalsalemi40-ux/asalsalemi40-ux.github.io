<div class="chapter">
<h2>The problem</h2>
<div class="body" markdown="1">
About one in four older adults falls every year. Standard walkers are cheap and familiar, but wheeled models are less stable, curbs and uneven floors are hard to cross, and lifting the frame tires weak hands and painful shoulders.
</div>
</div>

<div class="chapter">
<h2>What users need</h2>
<div class="body" markdown="1">
- Stability when standing still and when moving
- Brakes and locks that need little hand force
- A safe way over curbs and uneven ground, and a weight that can be lifted into a car
- A simple interface with clear touch or sound feedback
- A seat for resting and a safe way to stand back up
- Handles that adjust in height: research we reviewed puts the best handle height at about 48% of body height
</div>
</div>
