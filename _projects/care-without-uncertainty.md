<div class="chapter">
<h2>The challenge</h2>
<div class="body" markdown="1">
My research looked at mothers who carry the main responsibility for their children's health. Their situations differ: some are short of money, others of time, trust or someone to decide with. But they meet the same moment again and again, a sick child and a decision to make, often at night.

The design opportunity: reduce the uncertainty between noticing a child's symptoms and reaching appropriate care, especially at night, in urgent moments, or when money and transport are limited.
</div>
</div>

<div class="chapter">
<h2>Five personas</h2>
<div class="body" markdown="1">
I built five personas from the research. Each meets the same journey with a different main barrier.
</div>
</div>

<div class="personas">
<article class="persona">
<p class="persona-tag">The cautious caregiver</p>
<h3>Maryam, 33</h3>
<p class="persona-facts">Seamstress, two children, no insurance</p>
<blockquote>"If one of my children gets sick at night, my first thought is: how can I get help, and how much will it cost?"</blockquote>
<p><strong>Needs</strong> affordable, trustworthy care, clear fees and opening hours, and first guidance before a visit.</p>
<p><strong>Barriers</strong> no insurance, no transport at night, distrust after bad experiences.</p>
</article>
<article class="persona">
<p class="persona-tag">The unsupported decision-maker</p>
<h3>Masoumeh, 42</h3>
<p class="persona-facts">Teacher, one child, no insurance</p>
<blockquote>"Even when I can afford treatment, the responsibility of making the right decision alone is stressful."</blockquote>
<p><strong>Needs</strong> reassurance, simple guidance and a safe way to ask questions.</p>
<p><strong>Barriers</strong> no one to share the responsibility with.</p>
</article>
<article class="persona">
<p class="persona-tag">The time-sensitive caregiver</p>
<h3>Azadeh, 44</h3>
<p class="persona-facts">Pharmacy technician, one child, social security insurance</p>
<blockquote>"When time matters, long waiting times are exhausting, and high costs are still a concern."</blockquote>
<p><strong>Needs</strong> short waits, guidance around the clock, clear costs and follow-up.</p>
<p><strong>Barriers</strong> long queues, insurance limits, unpredictable costs.</p>
</article>
<article class="persona">
<p class="persona-tag">The cost-conscious planner</p>
<h3>Niusha, 34</h3>
<p class="persona-facts">Teacher, one child, supplementary insurance</p>
<blockquote>"In a sensitive moment, my biggest fear is not knowing what the treatment will cost."</blockquote>
<p><strong>Needs</strong> transparent, affordable options and advice online before a visit.</p>
<p><strong>Barriers</strong> unexpected costs, financial pressure.</p>
</article>
<article class="persona">
<p class="persona-tag">The reassurance seeker</p>
<h3>Elaheh, 30</h3>
<p class="persona-facts">Shop assistant, one child, no insurance</p>
<blockquote>"I want to make the right decision for my child, but I often feel alone and unsure during the process."</blockquote>
<p><strong>Needs</strong> credible sources, time to ask a doctor questions, an online consultation before travelling.</p>
<p><strong>Barriers</strong> information overload, fear of the wrong decision.</p>
</article>
</div>

<div class="chapter">
<h2>Five problem areas</h2>
<div class="body" markdown="1">
Across the personas and scenarios, the pain points fall into five connected areas.
</div>
</div>

<ol class="cards five">
<li><h3>Safety and access</h3><p>Fear of going out at night, no reliable transport, crowded or unfamiliar clinics.</p></li>
<li><h3>Trust and care quality</h3><p>Risk of the wrong treatment or self-medication, doubts about providers, a trusted doctor hard to reach.</p></li>
<li><h3>Psychological pressure</h3><p>Sole responsibility for the child, stress during illness, care and housework at the same time.</p></li>
<li><h3>Cost and resources</h3><p>High or unpredictable costs, little reliable health information, worry about illness spreading at home.</p></li>
<li><h3>Medication and continuity</h3><p>Anxiety about using medicine correctly, and treatment delayed or dropped because of cost or time.</p></li>
</ol>

<div class="chapter">
<h2>The concept</h2>
<div class="body" markdown="1">
A connected care companion. These are design directions from the research, not tested features.
</div>
</div>

<ol class="cards three">
<li><h3>Medical guidance, day and night</h3><p>A qualified professional or support line when a child becomes unwell, including outside clinic hours.</p></li>
<li><h3>Trusted care navigation</h3><p>Verified providers, nearby clinics and home-visit options, with clear service information.</p></li>
<li><h3>Costs up front</h3><p>Estimated fees, insurance coverage and lower-cost care paths before any decision.</p></li>
<li><h3>Prescriptions and medicine</h3><p>Verified digital prescriptions, clear instructions and pharmacy delivery.</p></li>
<li><h3>A health timeline for each child</h3><p>Appointments, medication schedules and care notes kept together.</p></li>
<li><h3>Human support, without judgement</h3><p>Step-by-step guidance and a respectful person to talk to, so no one faces it alone.</p></li>
</ol>
