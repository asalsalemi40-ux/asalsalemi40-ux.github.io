<div class="chapter">
<h2>The brief</h2>
<div class="body" markdown="1">
Be Natural is the English meaning of the brand's Persian name, Tabiei Bash. I studied the brand, its current two-bottle carrier and its competitors, then set out to design a pack that protects the bottles, is easy to carry and open, and keeps the brand honest and visible.
</div>
</div>

<div class="chapter">
<h2>Ten sketches, three finalists</h2>
<div class="body" markdown="1">
I explored ten concepts and scored them against the brief. Three made the shortlist: a stretched cube with a recessed grip, a one-piece triangular carrier that folds flat and assembles in seconds without glue, and a box with a rotating lid.
</div>
</div>
