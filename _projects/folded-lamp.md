<div class="chapter">
<h2>The idea</h2>
<div class="body" markdown="1">
Descriptive geometry is about moving between a form and its flat drawing. This lamp is built the same way: a faceted form developed into flat nets, then cut, scored and folded back into a volume.

A light inside shows through one opening and grazes the planes, so every facet takes a different tone.
