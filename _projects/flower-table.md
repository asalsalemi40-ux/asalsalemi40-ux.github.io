<div class="chapter">
<h2>The idea</h2>
<div class="body" markdown="1">
A side table read as a flower: the top is the bloom, the stem is the leg, and two round leaves along the stem become small shelves for a cup or a phone.
</div>
</div>

<div class="chapter">
<h2>Making it</h2>
<div class="body" markdown="1">
The petal outline and the circles were traced onto board, cut out with a jigsaw, sanded, and painted white and green.
</div>
