<div class="chapter">
<h2>The idea</h2>
<div class="body" markdown="1">
A desk lamp that reads as a figure: rectangular frames for the body and the legs, a box for the head, leaning over the desk the way you lean over a book. The light comes from where the face would be, straight down onto the page.
</div>
</div>

<div class="chapter">
<h2>Making it</h2>
<div class="body" markdown="1">
Every frame is built from straight wooden strips, cut to length and notched into half-lap joints, then glued, sanded and finished. The frames are joined to each other with bolts, and the box-shaped head holds a warm bulb.
</div>
</div>
