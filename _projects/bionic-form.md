<div class="chapter">
<h2>The theme</h2>
<div class="body" markdown="1">
Playing with bionic forms: taking a natural form, here a flower, and translating it into geometry. The bloom at the top, its open centre and the stem below are abstracted into faceted planes.
</div>
</div>

<div class="chapter">
<h2>The method</h2>
<div class="body" markdown="1">
Descriptive geometry is about moving between a form and its flat drawing. I developed each part as a flat net, then cut, scored and folded the white card back into a volume. A light placed inside for the photographs shows every facet as a different tone.
</div>
</div>
